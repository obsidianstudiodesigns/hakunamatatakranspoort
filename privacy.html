Privacy policy

Who we are

This website belongs to Hakuna Matata Kranspoort, a private holiday house at 82 Klapper Street, Kranspoort Vakansiedorp, Loskop, Mpumalanga. In this policy "we", "us" and "our" refer to Hakuna Matata Kranspoort. We are the responsible party for the personal information you share with us, as defined in the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

What we collect

This website has no user accounts and does not store any information on a server. When you use the booking form, the details you type in (your name, phone number, travel dates, number of guests and any message) are placed into a WhatsApp message or an email on your own device. Nothing is sent until you choose to send that message yourself.

If you contact us by WhatsApp, phone, email or Facebook, we receive the information you choose to share, such as your name, contact details and booking details.

How we use it

  • To answer your enquiry and confirm availability.
  • To manage your booking, payment and stay.
  • To contact you about your booking, for example with directions or arrival information.
  • To meet legal, tax or accounting obligations.

We do not sell your information and we do not use it for marketing without your consent.

Third parties

Messages you send us travel through the service you choose, such as WhatsApp (Meta) or your email provider, and are handled under their privacy policies. The map on our home page is provided by Google Maps, which may set its own cookies when it loads. Links to Facebook and Google Maps open their own websites.

Cookies

This website does not set its own cookies and does not use advertising or analytics trackers. Our fonts and images are served from this website directly.

How long we keep it

We keep booking information only for as long as we need it to manage your stay and to meet legal and tax requirements, after which it is deleted.

Keeping it safe

We take reasonable steps to protect the information you share with us and only use it for the purposes above.

Your rights

Under POPIA you may ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how it is used. To do so, email kranspoort.hakunamatata@gmail.com or WhatsApp 084 208 8639.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you may lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa at inforegulator.org.za.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it last changed.
