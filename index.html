Your bushveld getaway in Kranspoort.

Hakuna Matata is a wooden house built for slow days. Coffee on the deck at sunrise, long afternoons at the pool and evenings around the fire in the boma, with zebra wandering past the fence.

  • Sleeps 9Ideal for families and groups
  • 5 beds3 double and 2 single beds
  • Private poolScreened, with a shaded pergola
  • Lapa and bomaFor braais under the stars
  • Self-cateringFull kitchen and dining room
  • Deck with a viewOver the trees to the hills

Inside the house.

Wooden floors, high ceilings and calm, cool bedrooms. A full kitchen, a lounge to spread out in and a dining table made for long lunches.

Days are lived outside.

Big trees, red earth and wide views. Everything you need for a proper bush weekend is right outside the door.

The deck

Morning coffee above the treetops, with a wonderful view out to the hills.

The pool

Private and screened, with loungers in the sun and a shade sail for the hottest hours.

Evenings

Lanterns light the garden path once the sun goes down.

The lapa

A covered space with long wooden tables, so the braai goes ahead whatever the weather.

The upper deck

Climb the stairs for the best view of the trees and the hills beyond.

The garden

Aloes, shady trees and room for the kids to explore, right on the edge of the bush.

On the doorstep

Neighbours with stripes.

Kranspoort is a nature estate, so game moves freely between the houses. Zebra graze at dusk, antelope keep to the thickets and the birding starts at first light.

Hills, water and big skies.

Kranspoort is set in the hills around Loskop Dam. Go hiking or cycling, follow the river, and be back on the deck in time for sunset.

Warm fires, crisp mornings and starry nights.

The lapa and boma are yours for the whole stay. Light the fire, get the braai going and let the bush go quiet around you.
